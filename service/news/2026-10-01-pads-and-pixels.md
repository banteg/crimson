# Pads and Pixels

Version 0.11.0 of the Python port is out, and it's a big one.

Plug in a PlayStation, Xbox or Switch Pro pad and press any button: the game switches to twin-stick controls. Every menu works from the keyboard again too, as it did in the original. Tab moves, Enter presses, Escape goes back.

Fullscreen is borderless and keeps the original's shape at any size, with panels and text at their original pixel sizes. Alt+Enter toggles it.

Dozens of quests, perks, weapons and sounds were checked against the decompiled source and now behave exactly like the original. The shareware demo is gone, since the game ships as the full version only.
