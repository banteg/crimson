# 1.9.8 Too

Version 1.9.8, the Reflexive release from August 2003, now matches as well. Every one of its 850 game and engine functions compiles from the recovered source to the original's instructions. Four of them still differ by a single interchangeable byte, so by bytes it stands at 98.7%. Close enough.

That's two versions of Crimsonland, seven years apart, rebuilt from one source tree. The rest of the old versions are tracked on [decomp.dev](https://decomp.dev/banteg/crimson).
