# Instant Replay

Every run now saves a replay, and you can watch any of them. Pick a run on the high score screen, or click **Watch** next to one on [crimson.land](/), and it plays back in the game.

The viewer prepares a run in about a second and then seeks anywhere, backwards too, with every bloodstain right where it was. Each perk pick gets its own box along the scrub bar.

The best part: press **Take over** at any moment and the run is yours. Practise that one quest ending you can never get right, as many times as you like. Practice runs save nothing, so go wild.

Also new: bots rank on their own boards, next to the humans'. Tool-assisted runs finally have somewhere to go.
