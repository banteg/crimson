# Crimsonlang

Crimsonland now comes with a Crimsonlang. A quest is a short recipe: pick the starting weapon and the terrain, then arrange the original's enemies into timed waves, with loops and patterns. Switch off bonuses, perks or weapon drops if you're feeling mean.

Beat your quest, then publish it with `crimson quest publish`. Only a quest someone has cleared can go up, and the clear comes along as proof. Each one gets its own page under [Custom](/custom), with a preview of the arena, a leaderboard and the recipe to copy.

Can't be bothered to learn a language? <a href="/custom/llm.txt" data-native>crimson.land/custom/llm.txt</a> is the whole spec in one file. Hand it to your favourite AI and ask for something horrible.
