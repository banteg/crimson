# Internet Scores, Again

The original high score screen had **Update scores** and **Show internet scores** buttons. They haven't done anything in a long time. As of today they work again.

[crimson.land](/) is the new online leaderboard, with boards for Survival, every quest and every quest on hardcore. Tick **Ranked** in the Play Game menu, play, and your run uploads itself when its results screen closes. There's no sign-up: the game makes a key the first time you launch it.

Here's the twist: you don't send a score, you send the whole run. The server replays it from start to finish on the recovered game code, and only then does the score count. So no, you can't just type in a million.
