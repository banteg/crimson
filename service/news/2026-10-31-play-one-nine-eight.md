# Play 1.9.8

Two weeks ago 1.9.8 matched its original instructions. Now you can play it. Tick the **1.9.8** box next to Ranked in the Play Game panel, and your next run plays by the rules of the August 2003 release:

- Fire Bullets adds its fire shot beside each shot you fire instead of replacing it, and only for guns that fire projectiles.
- The Fire Bullets bonus never drops while someone holds a flamethrower or a rocket weapon.
- Only 64 projectiles fit on screen at once.
- Man Bomb, Fire Cough, Living Fortress and Tough Reloader don't exist yet.
- Angry Reloader fires its ring on every reload, short ones included.
- Radioactive kills lizards too.
- Typ-o Shooter's names come from 1.9.8's shorter word list.

Bugs that only 1.9.8 had are fixed, as 1.9.93's are. The [1.9.8 rules](https://crimson.banteg.xyz/rewrite/rules-1.9.8/) page lists every difference.

It plays in the browser at <a href="/play/" data-native>crimson.land/play</a>, in the desktop game and in the Python port. Ranked 1.9.8 runs have their own leaderboards: switch any board to **1.9.8** to see them. Untick the box and you're back on 1.9.93, no restart needed.

Stay tuned!
