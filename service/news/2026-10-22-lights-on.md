# Lights On

The Python port's RTX mode used to light just the projectiles. Now it lights the whole battlefield. Fire Bullets, Ion beams and explosions light up the ground, the creatures and you, and cast soft shadows. Fresh blood gleams when a beam passes over it, then dries to a matte crust. Smoke drifts in a real simulation and catches your laser sight.

Start the game with `crimson --rtx`, or type `rendermode rtx` in the console.
