# Full Blessing

This week I introduced the project in the 10tons Discord. Tero, who made Crimsonland and runs 10tons, replied: "you have our full blessing doing this".

Tero had already given permission to distribute the original game files, which is why everything here downloads them by itself instead of sending you to dig up your old CD.

To be clear, this is still a fan project and not an official 10tons one. But it's nice to know Crimsonland's creator is cheering us on.
