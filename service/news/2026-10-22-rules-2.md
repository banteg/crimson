# Rules 2

New runs now play by rules 2, a batch of gameplay and co-op fixes. Your old runs stay on the boards, and their replays still play and verify exactly as they were recorded. The boards show each run's rules version and can filter by it.

- In co-op, Fire Cough and Hot Tempered keep a separate timer for each player, so one player's proc no longer resets the other's.
- Dead players no longer count toward the quest life bonus, so overkill can't cost you quest time.
- Rocket kills pay out once, and Shock Chain and Jinxed only pick living targets.
- Fire Bullets converts every pellet of the Gauss Shotgun and the Multi-Plasma. Gauss fires bigger bursts, more slowly.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/banteg/crimson/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md#0210).

Stay tuned!
