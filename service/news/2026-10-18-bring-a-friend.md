# Bring a Friend

Crimsonland always had two-player co-op, as long as you both fit at one keyboard. Now you can play from two computers.

Click **Play co-op** on <a href="/play/" data-native>crimson.land/play</a> and send your friend the invite link. Once they join, play Survival or any quest together. Rollback netcode keeps your aim snappy under moderate lag, and a relay steps in when a direct connection won't work.

You share the experience, the level and the perks, but each of you has your own gun. Whoever opens the perk menu picks for both of you, so try not to argue about Fatal Lottery.

Co-op runs rank as a pair on their own boards, and their replays play back with the two-player camera.
