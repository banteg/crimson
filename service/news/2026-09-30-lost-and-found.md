# Lost and Found

Crimsonland went through a lot of versions: freeware releases all through 2002, then the Reflexive years from 2003 on. Most of the early ones vanished along with the download sites that hosted them.

This week 1.3.1, 1.8.7, 1.9.0 and 1.9.3 turned up on old magazine cover discs, thanks to [DiscMaster](https://discmaster.textfiles.com/) indexing every file on them, and 1.9.92 came out of an old Reflexive Arcade installer. The freeware 1.0.2, 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 are now being decompiled too, alongside 1.9.8 and 1.9.93.

Still missing: 1.1.1, 1.1.6, 1.1.7, 1.2.1, 1.2.2, 1.2.4, 1.2.5, 1.8.8, 1.8.9 and 1.9.91. If one of them is sitting on an old hard drive or a magazine cover disc in your closet, come say hi on [Telegram](https://t.me/+NEHzXtv5_fphZDYy). The [history page](https://crimson.banteg.xyz/re/static/reference/history/) has the full list.
